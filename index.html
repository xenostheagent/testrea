QUICK HELP

Your first groove

Click squares to switch drum hits on or off. Press Space to play, then change the tempo or swing while it runs.

Use the + next to a drum to replace its sample, or open Browser → Import drum sounds to add multiple samples as new sequencer tracks.

Use the top-bar Step and Piano Roll buttons to open either editor in the workspace. In the piano roll, choose an instrument, click to add notes, and drag notes or their edges to edit them.

Open Arrangement to add vocal takes or loops on separate tracks. Drag clips to line them up with your beat; playback combines all tracks at the project tempo.

Projects save in this browser. Use the project menu by the project name to save, rename, create, or switch projects. Export a full arrangement as WAV or download the drum pattern as JSON.
